# Business hypothesis: practical AI literacy

Revision 0.7 · September 28, 2026.

The shared core is an educational introduction to Chat / Work / Code. Matt's everyday and professional projects make the concepts concrete. A concise standalone guide, free community hour and separately scoped personal help are three delivery formats for those ideas.

The learner value is understanding products and agents, communicating a useful request, reviewing work and choosing an achievable next task. RoadScript provides professional context; a short Cozy Room case gives a visible software example. The ordinary promise is not a full game build in an hour.

The owner confirms daily calendar use of MgGriebZ.com and regular work use of RoadScript. His requirements/communication viewpoint belongs in the introduction as a personal perspective. Selected project evidence supports the story; it is not a client endorsement or a measured productivity claim.
