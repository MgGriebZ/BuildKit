Attribute that view to Matt; formal job titles, certifications and measured business outcomes have not been supplied.

## Three overlapping uses

- **Chat:** clarify an idea, understand something, draft or improve a message.
- **Work:** turn rough information into a reviewed brief, action table or plan. RoadScript illustrates communication with a team or stakeholders.
- **Code:** inspect and change software, then verify its behavior. The checked Cozy Room gives a small approachable example.

Chat / Work / Code is our teaching frame. ChatGPT Work is also a particular product surface; explain the distinction. Agents describe tool-using workflows available in different products. No single provider, model ladder or coding background is required to understand the hour.
