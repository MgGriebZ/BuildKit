## Time and handoff

Provisional capacity remains five owner hours/week and at most two sessions/week. A delivery week: three hours for two sessions/prep/handoff, one hour outreach, one hour material improvement/admin. Included support consumes the final block; reduce slots if it overruns. Community delivery replaces another block. Review after the first two paid sessions and again after three total delivered sessions: completed outcomes, total time, support burden and whether the pack helps clients repeat the milestone.

Before paid demand, cap owner landing-page polish at two hours; use a confirmed manual contact path if a new site blocks conversations. Account for one-time seminar production separately from the proposed 15-minute personalization allowance. Continue only to acceptance criteria. Workers return <=300 words: ticket, changed paths, observed checks, limitations and next dependency; no routine credit reports. The current requirements push is authorized; other purchases, outreach, production mutations and site publication require their own task authority.
