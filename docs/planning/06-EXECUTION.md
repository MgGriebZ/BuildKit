## Time and handoff

The owner intends a starting commitment of 5–10 hours/week as a minimum planning range, with room to expand. Five hours is not a ceiling, and there is no fixed two-session cap. Confirm actual calendar windows before promising dates. Example allocations below are planning scenarios once the reusable core exists, not bookings, revenue forecasts or a promised weekly event cadence:

| Work | 5-hour community-focused week | 10-hour mixed delivery week |
|---|---|---|
| Free class delivery | 1 hour | 1 hour |
| Event-specific prep, setup and travel | 1 hour | 1 hour |
| Host/learner conversations and outreach | 1 hour | 2 hours |
| Shared teaching material and rehearsal | 1 hour | 2 hours |

| Paid delivery, personalization and handoff | No booked slot assumed | 3 hours: two one-hour seminars plus 30 minutes each for prep/handoff |
| Total | 5 hours | 10 hours |

If there is no event or booking, reallocate that time to the core, rehearsal or conversations. Count actual travel and support; if either exceeds the allowance, expand the planned week only when the owner has the time, or reduce other commitments. Initial reusable seminar creation may take multiple weeks and is budgeted separately before treating these as delivery-week allocations. Review after the first free class, after the first two paid seminars and after the third paid seminar. Assess community usefulness separately from paid demand and support burden, then adjust event selection, slots and weekly commitment together.

Before paid demand, cap owner landing-page polish at two hours; use a confirmed manual contact path if a new site blocks conversations. Account for one-time seminar production separately from the proposed 15-minute personalization allowance. Continue only to acceptance criteria. Workers return <=300 words: ticket, changed paths, observed checks, limitations and next dependency; no routine credit reports.
