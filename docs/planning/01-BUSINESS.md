# Business hypothesis: an accessible AI introduction

Revision 0.6 · September 28, 2026. Product direction accepted; pricing and demand remain hypotheses.

## Offer

A compact, book-like introduction teaches adults how AI can help them build and change software, with Matt's live browser-game demonstration making the process tangible. The guide is independently useful; a community hour teaches the same core, and personal sessions add tailored help. See [PRD 15](15-PRD-AI-INTRO-LIVE-BUILD.md).

The first story starts with the prepared Cozy Room and adds a playable three-star feature using AI prompts, visible review, checks and repair. Show what was prepared and what changed live. A reader buys an organized explanation and repeatable learning path, not an assurance that every project will work in an hour.

Do not show a fictitious discount, "normally $50" valuation, lifetime-update promise or bestseller claim. No subscription to both AI vendors is required. Our product price and third-party tool costs are separately disclosed.

## Lightweight launch

A readable edition and dependable delivery are enough to test the product; an LMS, accounts/progress system and extensive video production are unnecessary. The optional recording follows rehearsal. An existing contact route and a useful free sample can support conversations while the paid edition is prepared.

For the guide: sample -> clear contents/prerequisites/price/terms -> confirmed purchase -> working delivery -> reader feedback. For personal help: inquiry -> scope/fit -> agreed slot/terms -> payment -> session -> handoff. Test delivery failures/refunds for the chosen channel before real sales.

## Evidence to collect

Ask a novice to read one chapter and explain or attempt the action without Matt. During the adult rehearsal, capture actual time, useful moments and confusion. Track voluntary sample interest, purchases, successful delivery, reading/task completion, support and refunds separately. Compliments and free attendance do not establish paid demand.

Review after the first rehearsal and first small group of actual readers, then decide whether price, scope or delivery needs changing. Avoid forecasting sales before evidence exists.

## Time and operating fit

Use the owner's expandable 5–10-hour/week starting range. Count one-time authoring/demo preparation separately from delivery, cloud/tool expenses, support and outreach. A one-time guide price cannot fund indefinite personal debugging; define edition updates and support explicitly.

[Operations](05-OPERATIONS.md) and [readiness](10-READINESS.md) cover the seller, offer-specific terms, tax classification and delivery questions. These are decisions to resolve for the actual products, not reasons to postpone drafting or a free rehearsal.
