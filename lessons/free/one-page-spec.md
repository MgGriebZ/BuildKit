# Plan one small event page

**Free class worksheet · fictional example · no account or paid tool required**

For a free community class: plan and check one small improvement together. No account or paid tool is needed. This shared exercise is not a personalized paid seminar or promise to build a full product.

## Ask and agree

Interview a learner or group. Note short answers:

- Who is it for, and what should visitors do?
- What facts are needed? What exists? What constraints matter?
- What one result can we show and check today?

**Goal:** Help visitors understand the event and next step.

**In scope:** One page section with event, date/time, place/format, audience, and next step.

**Out of scope:** Payments, accounts, registration, maps, live data, a full site, or personal-data collection. Ask about missing facts; never invent them.

**Milestone:** A visitor can tell what the event is, when/where it happens, and the next step.

## Worked example (fictional)

**Cedar Street Build-and-Share** — fictional free library event for adults curious about small projects and AI.

> Try a tiny project, see a working example, and leave with a next step. Saturday, October 17, 10–11 a.m., Cedar Street Library meeting room. Bring an idea; no purchase or account needed. Questions? Ask the host at the library information desk.

Practice copy only, not a real event. Check purpose, audience, time, place, and next step.

## Try the build loop

1. **Summarize:** “For [audience], help visitors [action]. Include [facts]; leave out [non-goals]. Ask about missing facts.”
2. **Plan:** “Suggest one change for [milestone], what stays out, and how to check it.” Agree before editing.
3. **Make and review:** Change one section. Ask: “What is missing, unclear, invented, or out of scope?” Check it together.
4. **Explain:** Say what changed, what you checked, what remains unknown, and one next step. Claim only checks you observed.

## If the request grows

Pause. Ask, “Which one change best supports our goal?” Put other ideas in **Later**. Shrink the example or stop at a useful plan; label unfinished work clearly.

## Done when

- Learner can identify audience, purpose, event facts, and next step.
- Agreed change is visible or recorded as a plan.
- Together, you checked missing facts, invented claims, personal data, and scope.
- Learner can explain what changed, what was checked, and what is unverified.
